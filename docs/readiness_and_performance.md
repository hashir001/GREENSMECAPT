# Readiness, performance and measures

## 1. Readiness observations

The research taxonomy describes conditions around and within an SME. To apply that causal model to a real applicant, the system first needs SME-specific observations. The readiness model records one state per subdriver:

- `enabling_evidence`
- `constraining_evidence`
- `mixed_evidence`
- `unknown`
- `not_applicable`
- `needs_review`

Evidence-based states require source identifiers. Missing evidence remains `unknown`; it is not converted to a neutral or zero judgment. The resulting profile has no scalar readiness score.

Once expert judgments are available, DEMATEL supplies each subdriver's influence given, influence received, prominence and cause/effect role. Those causal results can enrich the SME observations for investor explanation.

## 2. Environmental comparison context

A raw total describes quantity, not performance. For example, 100,000 kWh can be efficient for one production volume and inefficient for another. Before comparison, absolute measures need:

- a reporting period;
- a clear organisational and operational boundary;
- a denominator or activity level where intensity is appropriate;
- a baseline trend, target or sector/peer benchmark; and
- consistent unit and measurement definitions.

The metric catalogue declares these requirements. Electricity, water and total GHG values are explicitly marked `requires_comparison_context`. Waste diversion is also context-dependent because its denominator, material scope and treatment route must be consistent. Even a direct ratio such as renewable-energy percentage still requires a period and operational scope.

## 3. Financial separation

External finance availability, financing costs, guarantees, funder mandates and investor risk perception belong to readiness/context. ROA, revenue growth, liquidity, cash flow, leverage, project cost, requested amount and expected savings describe the SME and transaction. The two are related but must not be collapsed.

## 4. Calibration still required

The following remain unresolved until supplied and approved:

- expert directional pairwise judgments;
- the final fuzzy linguistic scale;
- performance criterion weights;
- sector benchmarks and target/reference profiles;
- ELECTRE indifference, preference and veto thresholds;
- evidence-strength thresholds; and
- any decision thresholds applied to readiness observations.
